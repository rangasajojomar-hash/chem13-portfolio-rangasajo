1. OVERVIEW OF AI UTILIZATION
In the development of this 10-molecule chemical card portfolio, an AI collaborator (Gemini by Google) was used as a quality-assurance, verification, and proofreading assistant. All visual elements, card designs in Canva, Ketcher/MolView structural drawings and file exports were created, executed, and submitted by the author.

2. SPECIFIC AREAS OF AI ASSISTANCE

A. Systematic Panel-by-Panel Verification:
   - Used AI to perform independent, step-by-step chemical audits across all 10 cards (Propane through Caffeine).
   - Verified that all four panels on every card (Molecular Formula, Lewis Structure, Condensed Formula, and Bond-Line Formula) maintained consistent connectivity, atom counts, and valence rules.

B. Lewis Structure & Valence Auditing:
   - Utilized AI to audit octet fulfillment and identify missing non-bonding electron pairs (lone pairs) on heteroatoms (Oxygen and Nitrogen).
   - Confirmed the addition of required lone pair dots in Canva (2 pairs / 4 dots for neutral Oxygen; 1 pair / 2 dots for neutral Nitrogen).

C. Formula & Nomenclature Corrections:
   - Verified IUPAC names, common names, and molecular formulas in card headers.
   - Identified and corrected errors in condensed structural formulas (e.g., updating Acetaminophen to CH3CONHC6H4OH and Caffeine to CH3NC(=O)N(CH3)C(=O)C=C1N(CH3)CH=N1).

D. Ketcher / MolView Bond-Line Rendering Guidance:
   - Used AI guidance to optimize Ketcher export settings (enabling 'Hetero' implicit hydrogen display, hiding terminal methyl carbons, and aligning bond angles).

3. STATEMENT OF ACADEMIC INTEGRITY
AI was utilized strictly as a consultative tool for verification and error-detection. All final chemical representations, graphic arrangements, file naming conventions, and repository assemblies reflect the original work and direct technical execution of the author.
